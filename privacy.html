R. My Ripple

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

My Ripple is a news app. It shows a short daily brief of Indian market news. It has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data, and it contains no analytics or advertising code.

What stays on your iPhone

  • The stocks you follow, your topics, saved stories, your daily goal and your reading streak.
  • These are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

Each day the app downloads the brief (text and pictures) from this website. Like any web request, this tells the web host your IP address; the host is GitHub Pages, whose own privacy statement applies. We do not receive or keep these requests.

Notifications

If you switch on the morning reminder, your iPhone schedules it locally. No push token is created and nothing is sent to us.

Links

"Read at source" opens the publisher's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

My Ripple is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. Questions: see the support page.
