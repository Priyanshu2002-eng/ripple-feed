Common questions

When does the brief arrive? Every morning at about 8:00 IST. Turn on the morning reminder in My Ripple to get a nudge at 8:15.

Where do the stories come from? Each one is a short summary, in our own words, of published reporting. Every card names the publisher and links to the original story.

Is this investment advice? No. My Ripple is a news app. It shows which listed companies a story mentions; it never says whether to buy or sell.

How do I reset the app? My Ripple tab, then "Redo setup". To erase everything, delete the app.

Publishers and companies
