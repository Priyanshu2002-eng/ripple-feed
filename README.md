# My Ripple: public feed

The public daily brief behind the My Ripple iPhone app, served by GitHub Pages at
https://priyanshu2002-eng.github.io/ripple-feed/

- `data/` the brief: `index.json` and `days/<date>.json` (news summaries, sources, companies named)
- `art/`, `co/photo/` openly licensed pictures, credited in `data/credits.json`
- `index.html`, `privacy.html`, `support.html` the app's public pages

News only: no buy/sell labels, no prices, no company logos. Not investment advice.
Updated every morning at about 8:00 IST. Questions or corrections: open an issue.
